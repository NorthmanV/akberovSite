Privacy Policy

Ruslan Akberov ("I", "me", or "my") operates the mobile applications (the "Service"). This page informs you of my policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use my Service.

Information Collection and Storage

I do not collect, store, or share your personal information on any external servers owned or managed by me. Your data remains strictly on your device and within your personal storage ecosystem.

iCloud Data Synchronization

Some features of the Service may use Apple's CloudKit / iCloud services to sync your data across your devices:

  • Your data is stored locally on your device and in your private Apple iCloud container.
  • I do not have access to your personal data synced via iCloud.
  • Data synchronization is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

If you make in-app purchases or subscribe to premium features:

  • All financial transactions and payment details are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit and the App Store.
  • I do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

Third-Party Services

The Service relies on Apple iOS framework infrastructure. I encourage you to review Apple’s Privacy Policy to understand how they handle device-level data.

Children's Privacy

I do not collect any personal data from children. I strongly encourage parents and guardians to monitor their children's digital activities and keep an eye on their app usage to ensure a safe experience.
